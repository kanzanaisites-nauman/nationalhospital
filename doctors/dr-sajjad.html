About

About Dr. Sajjad Hussain

Dr. Sajjad Hussain is an experienced Neurosurgeon (MBBS, FCPS) practicing at National Hospital, Bahawalnagar, specializing in the diagnosis and surgical treatment of conditions affecting the brain, spine, and nervous system. He is trained in complex spine surgery and minimally invasive neurosurgical techniques, allowing patients to benefit from precise treatment with reduced surgical trauma and faster recovery.

Dr. Hussain is committed to providing patients with clear, compassionate guidance through what can be a stressful and complex area of medicine — from initial diagnosis through surgery and post-operative recovery.
