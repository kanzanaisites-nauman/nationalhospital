About

About Dr. Zareen Amjad

Dr. Zareen Amjad is an experienced Obstetrician and Gynecologist (MBBS, FCPS) practicing at National Hospital, Bahawalnagar. She provides compassionate, comprehensive care for women at every stage of life — from adolescence through pregnancy, motherhood, and menopause.

Dr. Amjad's practice covers the full spectrum of women's health, including antenatal care for normal and high-risk pregnancies, menstrual and hormonal disorders, family planning, infertility evaluation, and routine gynecological care, with a focus on making every patient feel heard, respected, and well cared for.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Dr. Amjad treats a wide range of women's health concerns, including pregnancy care, menstrual problems, PCOS and hormonal imbalances, infertility, menopause symptoms, and routine gynecological issues.

It's recommended to begin antenatal checkups as soon as pregnancy is confirmed, ideally within the first trimester, to ensure early monitoring and a healthy pregnancy.

A pregnancy may be considered high-risk due to factors like maternal age, pre-existing health conditions, multiple pregnancies, or complications arising during pregnancy. Dr. Amjad can assess your individual risk factors and provide appropriate care.

Common signs include irregular periods, excess hair growth, acne, weight gain, and difficulty conceiving. Dr. Amjad can evaluate your symptoms and recommend appropriate testing and treatment.

There are several contraception and family planning methods available. Dr. Amjad can discuss the options suited to your health, lifestyle, and future family plans.

Couples who have been trying to conceive for a year without success (or six months if the woman is over 35) are generally advised to seek an infertility evaluation.

Common symptoms include irregular periods, hot flashes, mood changes, and sleep disturbances. Dr. Amjad can help manage these symptoms and address related health concerns.

Routine gynecological checkups are generally recommended annually, or as advised based on your age, health history, and any ongoing concerns.
