This policy explains what information National Hospital Bahawalnagar collects through this website, why we collect it, and how it is used. We collect only what is needed to respond to your appointment request and to communicate with you about it.

What we collect

When you use the appointment booking form on our website, we collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your mobile phone number
  • The department/clinic you'd like to visit
  • Your preferred appointment date
  • Any notes you choose to add for our care desk

We do not collect payment information, medical history, or any sensitive health data through this website.

How we use it

Information submitted through the booking form is used only to:

  • Contact you by phone to confirm your appointment
  • Prepare our care desk and the relevant department for your visit

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How it's delivered and stored

This website sends appointment form submissions directly to the hospital's email inbox using a third-party form delivery service. We do not maintain a separate patient database from this website — submissions exist only as emails received by our staff, which are handled according to our internal record-keeping practices.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages are most useful, so we can improve the site. This does not involve tracking you across other websites or selling data to advertisers.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you from a booking submission, and request that we delete it, by contacting us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our services change. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
