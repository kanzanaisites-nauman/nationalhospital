Booking Form Delivery

Important: without this set up, the appointment form on your site shows patients a confirmation message, but nobody at the hospital actually receives it. Setting this up takes 2 minutes and requires no coding or backend server:

1. Go to web3forms.com and enter the hospital's email address
2. You'll instantly get a free "Access Key" — no account signup needed
3. Paste that key below, then Generate & Export as usual
4. Every booking form submission will now arrive directly in that inbox
